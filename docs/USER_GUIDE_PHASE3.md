# Phase 3 user guide

## Choose how answers are explained

Select **Simple**, **Detailed**, or **Advocate-style** in settings. The amount of detail changes, but legal meaning, source citations, warnings, and safety rules do not. You can save your language and preferred export format; case facts are not saved as global preferences.

## Chat, voice, and follow-ups

Use text or the microphone in the mobile-friendly chat. Hindi, Hinglish, and other configured Indian languages can use voice input/output when the speech provider is configured. The assistant asks one important missing question at a time and avoids facts already found in the chat or uploaded documents.

Voice transcription can be wrong. If voice was used to collect draft facts, review the displayed facts and explicitly confirm them. Export remains blocked until confirmation. Confirmation does not file or submit the document.

## Use every feature from chat

There are no separate feature pages or workflow buttons. Describe the result
you want in the normal chat, for example: "prepare this document for
notarization", "review this agreement for missing clauses", "compare these
two documents", "show my saved drafts", or "create a case". The assistant
asks only for missing details, shows progress inside its reply, and asks for
confirmation before a destructive or legally significant action.

Say "what can you do?" at any time for the current capability overview. You
can also say "Hindi mein batao", "reply in English", or "Hinglish mein
samjhao" during an unfinished task; the task and facts already collected are
preserved.

## Official form assistance

Police, cybercrime, RTI, and consumer workflows can pre-fill structured
fields in the conversation. The assistant highlights missing information for
review. Correct every field and annexure before confirming. The product does
not send a form to a police station, court, commission, portal, or other
authority; any future submission action must ask for separate explicit
authorization and confirmation.

## Cases, drafts, search, and downloads

Use the sidebar only to reopen previous conversations or change minimal
settings. Ask in chat to list or open your cases, drafts, jobs, and downloads.
Search finds content only within your own chats and indexed documents. Draft
review supports facts, sources, assumptions, missing information, annexures,
versions, comparison, edit/regenerate/translate, duplicate, delete, and
PDF/DOCX/TXT/RTF export.

Contradictory facts are never silently selected. Resolve conflicts before final export. Jurisdiction results are tentative and show **verify before filing** warnings.

## Privacy and lawyer escalation

Each case is isolated. Sensitive facts from one case are not used in another. Preferences and case data are editable/deletable, and account deletion removes owner-scoped Phase 3 records and generated download files when present.

For complex or high-risk matters, the assistant can prepare a lawyer-ready summary containing facts, timeline, key documents, draft status, and unanswered questions. You choose whether to download or share it; the product sends nothing automatically.

Use feedback controls to report **helpful**, **wrong language**, **wrong law**, **missing source**, or **unsafe draft**. Do not rely on an AI-generated draft as a substitute for review by a qualified advocate, especially where rights, limitation periods, arrest, large losses, or court filing are involved.
